Biography

About Mary Clare

At eighteen, Mary Clare is a rising Texas country artist blending today's fresh country sound with the heart of the classics. A Texas native, she has been singing since before she could talk, and has spent the past decade performing on stages across the country, developing a style rooted in authentic storytelling and a lifelong love of country music.

Since taking the stage at eight years old, she has performed throughout Texas as well as in Branson, Nashville, Oklahoma, Washington D.C., and New York. Along the way, she has opened for some of the most respected names in the genre and shared the stage in duets with Jennifer Nettles of Sugarland and Linda Davis, known for her iconic duet with Reba McEntire, “Does He Love You,” and as the mother of Lady A's Hillary Scott. She has recorded original music in Nashville twice, first at eleven years old and again at seventeen.

Influenced by both classic country legends and today's hitmakers, Mary Clare performs a blend of modern and traditional country while accompanying herself on guitar and piano. She is a dedicated songwriter, passionate about honest stories that connect with audiences of every age, bringing genuine stage presence and heartfelt vocals to festivals, fairs, songwriter rounds, and community events alike.
